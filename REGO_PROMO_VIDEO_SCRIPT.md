**Runtime:** 60 seconds · **Format:** 16:9 for YouTube/web, 9:16 vertical for Reels/TikTok/Status
**Voice:** warm, confident, local — not a corporate announcer
**Music:** slow build, traditional GB instrument (rubab or surnai) opening → modern beat drop at 0:10

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Shoot list — footage you actually need.** Roughly 25 shots. The drone work over Skardu, Deosai and Attabad is worth hiring properly; the rest can be captured on a good phone over two or three days around Skardu.

**Use your own providers.** Film in listings that are actually on Rego, and credit them on screen. It costs nothing, it gives them a reason to share the video, and it means every frame is honest — a traveller who books what they saw gets what they saw.

**Do not use stock footage of other mountain ranges.** It is tempting and it is a trap. People who know GB will spot Swiss alps or Nepali trails immediately, and the credibility loss outweighs the production polish.

**Claims to avoid.** Do not say "thousands of listings", "best prices guaranteed", or "24/7 support" unless each is literally true today. A promo that oversells is the fastest way to lose the trust that a marketplace runs on — and in a region this small, word travels.

**The one line to keep if you cut everything else:**
*"Rego — your complete Gilgit-Baltistan tourism marketplace."*
