# Rego — 15-Second Launch Promo (CapCut Storyboard)

**Goal:** Hype the launch of Rego, the all-in-one tourism marketplace for Gilgit-Baltistan.
**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical) for Reels / TikTok / Shorts. (Also export a 1:1 and 16:9 later.)
**Brand colors:** Forest green `#14513B` · Gold `#E0A82E` · White `#FFFFFF`
**Font:** Montserrat (Bold for headlines, Medium for sub-text) — matches the website.
**Vibe:** Cinematic mountains → fast, modern app reveal → strong CTA.
**Music:** Uplifting cinematic build with a beat drop at ~0:07 (CapCut → Audio → search "epic travel" / "inspiring drop"). Add one "whoosh" transition SFX and one "click/pop" SFX.

## Clip-by-clip build (with exact CapCut settings)

### SCENE 1 — HOOK (0.0–3.0s)
- **Footage:** 1 aerial drone shot of Karakoram/Hunza (3s). Free sources: Pexels, Pixabay (search "Hunza", "Karakoram", "mountains aerial").
- **Clip animation:** Select clip → **Animation → Group → "Zoom in slow"** (or add a manual keyframe: scale 100% → 108% over 3s for a slow push-in).
- **Filter:** **Filters → Landscape → "Vivid" or "Warm"** at ~40% for punchy greens/blues.
- **Text:** "GILGIT-BALTISTAN AWAITS" — Montserrat Bold, white, letter-spacing wide.
  - Text **In animation: "Rise"** (0.4s). **Out animation: "Fade"** (0.3s).
  - Position lower third.
- **Overlay:** Add a subtle dark gradient at the bottom (Overlay → add a black-to-transparent PNG, or lower clip brightness at bottom) so text is readable.
- **SFX:** soft ambient wind + music intro.

### SCENE 2 — THE PROBLEM (3.0–5.0s)
- **Footage:** 3 quick clips (~0.66s each) — a hotel room, a jeep on a mountain road, a guide/traveler. Fast energy.
- **Transitions between the 3:** **Transitions → "Pull in" or "Zoom"** (0.1–0.2s each). Keep them SNAPPY.
- **Text:** "One trip. Too many bookings?" — white, centered.
  - **In: "Typewriter"** or **"Wipe"**. Slight shake to feel chaotic.
- **Speed:** Right-click clips → speed 1.2×–1.5× to feel hectic (reinforces the "too complicated" problem).
- **SFX:** rising tension riser (Audio → "riser"/"whoosh").

### SCENE 3 — BRAND REVEAL / BEAT DROP (5.0–7.0s) ⭐ the money moment
- **Transition INTO this:** Full **white or gold flash** — add a solid gold clip (Overlay → color) for 3 frames with **opacity keyframes 0→100→0**, OR use **Transitions → "White flash"**. Land the flash exactly on the music beat drop.
- **Background:** Solid forest-green `#14513B` (Overlay → Color board) or a blurred mountain.
- **Logo:** Drop your **Rego logo PNG** (transparent).
  - **Animation In: "Scale/Bounce"** (logo pops from 0% → 110% → 100%). Add a tiny gold glow (Overlay → duplicate logo, blur it, gold tint, behind main logo).
- **Text under logo:** "Book your entire trip in one place" — gold `#E0A82E`, Montserrat Medium. **In: "Fade" + "Rise"**, delayed 0.3s after logo.
- **SFX:** deep bass drop + a "shine/sparkle" sound.

### SCENE 4 — WHAT YOU GET (7.0–11.0s)
- **Footage:** Screen-record the Rego site/app (scroll the homepage: hotels → transport → guides → tours → food). Keep it smooth; record at 60fps if possible.
  - Put the screen recording inside a **phone mockup frame** (search CapCut Stickers "phone frame", or overlay a phone PNG) for a premium app feel.
- **Clip animation:** phone slides up — **Animation → In → "Slide up"**.
- **Text — animated word list** (each word pops in on the beat):
  - "HOTELS" → "JEEPS" → "GUIDES" → "TOURS" → "FOOD"
  - Each word its own text layer, ~0.6s apart, **In: "Pop"/"Bounce"**, **Out: "Zoom out"**. Gold + white alternating.
- **Motion:** add small **keyframe** movement to the phone (drift left→right) so it's never static.
- **SFX:** UI "pop/click" on each word.

### SCENE 5 — TRUST + LAUNCH (11.0–13.5s)
- **Footage:** Close-up of a listing card with the **gold verified tick** (screen-record from your site) or a montage of 2–3 provider cards.
- **Sticker:** animated **gold check/verified badge** (CapCut Stickers → "verified" / "check") popping in with **"Bounce"**.
- **Text:** "Verified providers." then "NOW LIVE." — "NOW LIVE" big, gold, **In: "Punch/Scale"** with a subtle shake.
- **Effect:** add **Effects → "Gold particles" / "Sparkle"** lightly over the badge.

### SCENE 6 — CTA END CARD (13.5–15.0s)
- **Background:** forest green with a faint mountain silhouette.
- **Logo** center + **URL/handle** below: e.g. `rego.pk` and `@rego`.
- **Text:** "Plan smarter. Travel better." (small) + **"Visit rego.pk today →"** button-style.
  - Button: rounded gold rectangle (Overlay → shape/sticker) with dark text; **In: "Rise"**, gentle pulse (scale 100%↔104% loop via keyframes).
- **Hold** the end card 1.5s so viewers can read/screenshot.
- **Audio:** let music resolve; end clean (don't cut mid-beat — use **Fade out 0.3s** on audio).

## CapCut cheat-sheet (where each control lives)
- **Clip animations:** select clip → bottom toolbar **Animation** → *In / Out / Group (loop)*.
- **Text animations:** select text → **Animation** → *In / Out / Loop*. Good picks: Rise, Typewriter, Pop, Bounce, Zoom.
- **Transitions:** tap the little □ between two clips → pick (White flash, Pull in, Zoom, Whoosh). Keep 0.1–0.3s.
- **Keyframes** (for custom motion): move playhead → tap the ◆ diamond → change scale/position → move playhead → change again. CapCut fills the motion between.
- **Beat markers:** select music → **Beats → Auto-generate**. Snap your cuts/word pops to the dots.
- **Effects:** **Effects → Video effects** → search "flash", "gold particle", "sparkle", "shake".
- **Overlays / mockups:** **Overlay → Add overlay** for phone frame, gradients, color boards.

## Timing rule of thumb
15s is short — the beat drop/logo reveal MUST land by ~0:06 or it feels slow. Front-load the wow.
