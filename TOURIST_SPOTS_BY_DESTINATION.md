# Tourist Spots by Destination — Gilgit-Baltistan

Suggested "Top Attractions" content for each destination page on Rego.
Ordered by how much travellers actually search for them — put the first
3–4 on the destination page, keep the rest for a "More to explore" list.

---

## SKARDU (Baltistan)
*The most-searched GB destination. Base for K2 and Deosai.*

| Spot | Why it matters | Type |
|---|---|---|
| **Shangrila / Lower Kachura Lake** | The iconic red-roof resort lake — GB's most photographed spot | Lake |
| **Upper Kachura Lake** | Deep turquoise, boating, quieter than Lower | Lake |
| **Satpara Lake** | On the road to Deosai, reservoir views | Lake |
| **Sarfaranga Cold Desert** | World's highest cold desert, jeep rally venue | Desert |
| **Katpana Desert** | Sand dunes against snow peaks | Desert |
| **Kharpocho Fort** | Hilltop fort with full valley view | Heritage |
| **Basho Valley** | Pine forest, camping, waterfalls | Valley |
| **Soq Valley** | Green meadows, day trip from town | Valley |
| **Blind Lake (Shigar side)** | Hidden lake, short trek | Lake |
| **Manthokha Waterfall** | 180ft falls, Kharmang — popular day trip | Waterfall |

---

## HUNZA (Karimabad)
*Highest-value destination for hotels and tours.*

| Spot | Why it matters | Type |
|---|---|---|
| **Baltit Fort** | 700-year-old UNESCO-listed fort, the face of Hunza | Heritage |
| **Altit Fort** | Older than Baltit, royal garden | Heritage |
| **Attabad Lake** | Bright blue lake formed by the 2010 landslide, boating | Lake |
| **Passu Cones** | The cathedral-like peaks everyone photographs | Mountain |
| **Eagle's Nest (Duikar)** | Best sunrise/sunset viewpoint in GB | Viewpoint |
| **Hussaini Suspension Bridge** | Famous (terrifying) rope bridge | Adventure |
| **Khunjerab Pass** | Highest paved border crossing, 4,693m — China border | Pass |
| **Borith Lake** | Migratory birds, quiet stop near Passu | Lake |
| **Passu & Batura Glacier** | Accessible glacier walks | Glacier |
| **Rakaposhi View Point** | Roadside café facing a 7,788m peak | Viewpoint |

---

## NAGAR
*Often bundled with Hunza — worth its own page.*

- **Hopper Valley & Hopper Glacier** — black glacier, dramatic bowl valley
- **Rakaposhi Base Camp (via Minapin)** — the most doable base-camp trek in GB
- **Rush Lake** — one of the highest alpine lakes in the world (trek)
- **Nagar Fort / Khyber village**

---

## GILGIT (City)
*Transport hub — most visitors pass through.*

- **Naltar Valley & the three lakes** — Blue/Green/Pink lakes, jeep track, ski slope
- **Kargah Buddha** — 7th-century rock-carved Buddha
- **Three Mountains Junction Point (Juglot)** — where Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindukush meet
- **Chinar Bagh & Gilgit Bridge** — riverside, city walk
- **Danyore Suspension Bridge & rock inscriptions**

---

## GHIZER
*Underrated — great for a "hidden GB" angle.*

- **Phander Lake & Valley** — trout fishing, the "mini Switzerland" shot
- **Khalti Lake** — freezes in winter, roadside
- **Shandur Pass** — world's highest polo ground (July festival)
- **Handrap Lake** — trek beyond Phander
- **Yasin & Gupis valleys**

---

## ASTORE
*Gateway to Nanga Parbat's Rupal face and Deosai's south gate.*

- **Rama Lake & Rama Meadows** — pine forest, Nanga Parbat backdrop
- **Rupal Valley** — the world's highest mountain face (4,600m Rupal Face)
- **Minimarg & Domel** — near the LoC, permit area, stunning
- **Chilam** — Deosai's southern entrance
- **Tarishing** — Rupal base camp trailhead

---

## DIAMER
*Fairy Meadows is the star.*

- **Fairy Meadows** — the classic Nanga Parbat viewpoint and campsite
- **Nanga Parbat Base Camp / Beyal Camp** — day trek from Fairy Meadows
- **Babusar Top** — 4,173m pass, opens summer only
- **Raikot Bridge** — where the famous jeep track starts
- **Tato Village** — last road point before the Fairy Meadows walk

---

## SHIGAR
- **Shigar Fort (Fong Khar)** — restored palace, now a heritage hotel
- **Amburiq Mosque** — 14th-century wooden mosque
- **Askole** — last village before K2 / Baltoro Glacier
- **Blind Lake**

---

## KHAPLU (Ghanche)
- **Khaplu Palace (Yabgo Khar)** — beautifully restored royal residence
- **Chaqchan Mosque** — 700-year-old carved wooden mosque
- **Hushe Valley** — gateway to Masherbrum and K6/K7
- **Saling & Thalley Valley** — orchards, quiet homestay country
- **Machulu viewpoint**

---

## RONDU / ROUNDU
*Between Skardu and Gilgit along the Indus. Almost no tourism coverage —
your biggest content gap and easiest ranking opportunity.*

- **Tormik Valley** — long side valley running towards the Haramosh range
- **Thorsay Bilamik Meadows** — high summer meadows above Bilamik
- **Talu Broq** — traditional Balti summer pasture (*broq* = pasture)
- **Hango Valley** — quiet villages and walking trails off the main road
- **Stak Nala** — opens off the Skardu–Gilgit road towards Haramosh / Stak La
- **Rondu Gorge** — the sheer Indus gorge the Skardu road threads through
- **Mendi** — riverside village, natural halfway stop

---

## GOJAL (Upper Hunza)
- **Shimshal Valley** — highest settled valley in Pakistan
- **Chapursan Valley** — remote, runs to the Afghan Wakhan border
- **Batura Glacier** — among the longest glaciers outside the poles
- **Misgar** — old Silk Road side valley
- **Khunjerab National Park** — snow leopard and Marco Polo sheep

---

## CHILAS
- **Chilas Rock Carvings** — thousands of petroglyphs along the Indus
- **Babusar Top** — the summer crossing into Kaghan
- **Thak Nala** — quiet side valley with more carvings
- **Raikot Bridge** — turn-off for Fairy Meadows

---

## DAREL & TANGIR
- **Darel Valley** — dense pine forest, one of the greenest valleys in Diamer
- **Tangir Valley** — alpine pastures at its head
- **Shatial Rock Carvings** — major Silk Road inscription site

---

## GUPIS-YASIN
- **Yasin Valley** — green terraces, old polo grounds, route to Darkot
- **Khalti Lake** — freezes solid in winter, beside the road
- **Darkot Pass** — historic crossing towards Broghil and the Wakhan
- **Ghizer River** — trout water the length of the valley

---

## KHARMANG
- **Manthokha Waterfall** — the big one, 180ft
- **Khamosh Waterfall** — narrow gorge falls
- **Kharmang valley terraces**

---

## DEOSAI NATIONAL PARK
*Seasonal (roughly late June – September).*

- **Sheosar Lake** — "Blind Lake", Nanga Parbat on the horizon
- **Bara Pani** — the classic camping/photo stop
- **Kala Pani** — river crossing point
- **Himalayan brown bear** — the park's conservation story
- **Wildflower season** — July, the plateau turns into a carpet

---

## ⚠️ Note on Naran

**Naran / Kaghan Valley is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, not Gilgit-Baltistan.**
It's currently listed as a GB destination on Rego. Options:

1. Keep it (travellers do route Naran → Babusar → GB) but label it
   *"Gateway to GB — Kaghan Valley, KP"* rather than GB.
2. Move it to a separate "On the way" group.

If you keep it, the spots are: **Saif-ul-Malook Lake**, **Lulusar Lake**,
**Babusar Top**, **Ansoo Lake** (trek), **Shogran / Siri Paye**.

## How to use these

For each destination page, show **4–6 spots** as cards with:
- Photo
- Name
- One line ("Bright blue lake formed by the 2010 landslide")
- Type badge (Lake / Fort / Viewpoint / Trek / Glacier)
- Optional: best season, distance from the town centre

Then link each spot to the relevant Rego services — *"Hotels near Attabad Lake"*,
*"Jeeps to Fairy Meadows"* — which turns content into bookings and gives you
strong long-tail SEO pages.
